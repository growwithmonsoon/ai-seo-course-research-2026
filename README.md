# AI SEO Course Research 2026

This repository accompanies **[AISEOcourse.pro](https://aiseocourse.pro/)**, a source-backed database researching current AI SEO, GEO and AEO training.

The project tracks public evidence around course format, price, topic coverage, GEO/AEO, LLM citations, entity SEO, AI visibility measurement and support. Missing evidence remains unknown.

**Interactive database:** https://aiseocourse.pro/

## Research principles

- Use public primary sources where available.
- Record verification dates and preserve visible uncertainty.
- Do not convert missing public evidence into a negative claim.
- Separate AI-assisted conventional SEO from training focused on AI-search visibility.
- Keep courses, memberships, cohorts, communities and guides identified as different product types.
